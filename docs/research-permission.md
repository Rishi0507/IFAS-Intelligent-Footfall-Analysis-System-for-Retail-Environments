# Permission to use CCTV footage for research

A template for the written permission task A3 needs from each store
([research-plan.md](research-plan.md)). Fill in the brackets, print two copies, and have the store
owner or manager sign both. The store keeps one. Scan the other, keep the scan on the team's
Drive, and record where it is in the retail clip table of [research-data.md](research-data.md).

Before you ask a store, check with your institute whether this study needs approval from its
ethics committee, and get that first if it does. This template is a starting point for that
conversation. It is not legal advice.

---

**[Institute name], [Department]**

**Request to use recorded CCTV footage in a research study**

Dear [name of owner or manager],

We are students at [institute], working under [guide's name], on a system that counts shoppers
and estimates their age group and gender from a shop's own CCTV. We would like to use some of your
recorded footage to measure how accurate the system is. This letter says what we would take, what
we would do with it and what we would never do.

**What we would take.** Recordings from [camera or cameras] on [dates or date range], about
[length] in total. We use footage your cameras already record. We install no camera and record
nothing new.

**What we do with it.**

1. We run our software on the footage on our own computers. It finds each person and cuts out
   small pictures of them.
2. Two of us look at those pictures and write down each person's apparent age group and gender.
   We use these notes only to check the software's answers.
3. We report totals only, such as "the software was right for 84% of 200 people".

**What we never do.**

* We never try to find out who anyone is. We record no names and match no faces to anyone.
* We never publish, share or show a picture or video in which a person can be recognised. Figures
  in our report show charts and numbers only.
* We never give the footage to anyone outside the project team.
* We never use the footage for anything other than this study.

**How long we keep it.** The footage, the small pictures and our notes stay on [where: e.g.
"two password-protected laptops and the team's private Drive folder"]. We delete all of them by
[date], or sooner if you ask.

**Your choice.** Saying no has no consequence. You can withdraw this permission at any time by
telling [contact]. We then delete everything taken from your store, and leave it out of any
result we have not yet published.

**Shoppers.** Your store displays a notice that CCTV is in use: [yes / no]. We do not use footage
from any area where shoppers would expect privacy, such as trial rooms or washrooms. We do not
label anyone who looks under 16.

Contact: [name, phone, email]. Guide: [name, email].
